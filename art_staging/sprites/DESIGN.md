# Character grammar

The Visitor's scarf, plum coat, and satchel carry the silhouette. Fig's ears, golden eyes, upright pose and burgundy tie identify the receptionist at small scale. Thimble has glasses and an open book; Crumb a helmet and needle; Mabel a hat, apron and cup; the Auditor a taller coat-and-paper silhouette.

All visible sprite pixels use the shared 16-color palette, with no antialiasing and only 0/255 alpha. Review at native and integer nearest-neighbor scales. One transparent pixel separates frames from their cell edges. Larger UI portraits are separately authored rather than enlarged world sprites.

Current review risks are restrained walking motion, dark limb contrast on plum floors, and limited detail in static NPC studies. No claim of user approval or runtime playtesting is made.
