# UI visual rules

Quiet dark-plum panels, paper text, restrained amber ornament and blush focus. Gothic details belong to borders and headings; dialogue uses licensed DejaVu Sans at 24px in the desktop study and 18px in the compact study. Notebook body text is 22px/18px respectively. Labels and control captions are at least 16px; small review footers outside the product study are 14px on compact exports.

Focus uses both an arrow and a border. The Here has a sun icon and a label; The Almost has a moon icon available in the icon sheet. Do not depend on color alone. Native icons remain integer-scaled; body text renders at display resolution and may be antialiased.

Nine-slice metadata uses 8px source margins. Keep text outside production panel textures. The 80×80 portrait frame wraps a 64×64 portrait with 8px margins. Do not stretch portrait art to fit arbitrary aspect ratios.

The notebook study records only the supplied Guestbook clue. It presents two acquired items in six spaces; empty spaces are visible and distinct. The sample has no save state, input behavior, inventory implementation or runtime accessibility claim.

Font files and redistribution terms are in `../branding/fonts/`. Source geometry is original; supplied reference boards were not cropped into these assets.
