# Shared visual direction

Warm, lived-in Gothic rooms, expressive small silhouettes, plum shadows, amber candles, and restrained blush accents. The Almost adds cool light and new shapes while preserving architecture. Native pixel assets use exact palette colors and integer pixels; typography and conceptual illustration have separate rendering rules.

Use text and shape as well as color to distinguish modes and focus. Label review images as art mockups. The exact title is **The House That Ate Tomorrow** and tagline is **Tomorrow is missing. The cat denies everything.** Neither is baked into the scene background source.
