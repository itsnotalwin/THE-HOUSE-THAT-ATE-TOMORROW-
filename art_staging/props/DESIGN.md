# Clues before typography

The guestbook changes silhouette through floating pages in The Almost. Its small marks are illustration, not text. Both nameplates keep an empty center; the future clue “THE VISITOR” needs a separate readable text layer. Amber and timber become moon cream and lavender while retained outlines keep the object recognizable.

Every native PNG has binary straight alpha and uses the shared palette. The ghost is an opaque pixel silhouette, not a runtime translucency effect. These are unapproved static art samples; animation, interaction and collision remain unimplemented.
