# Wordmark design

Cream title lines and blush “Tomorrow” use a restrained storybook serif with a near-midnight clock, crooked roof fragments, leaf flourishes, and a small cat cameo. Ornaments leave the letterforms clear. The exact title reads **The House That Ate Tomorrow**. The separate two-line tagline reads **Tomorrow is missing. The cat denies everything.**

This is an original font-backed lockup, not a claim of custom-drawn type design. DejaVu Serif Bold supplies title letterforms; DejaVu Sans supplies the tagline. Both unmodified fonts are redistributed with their license in `fonts/`. Original ornament/layout work was authored as vectors for this project; no reference game assets or supplied concept crops were used.

Branding uses antialiasing and smooth display resampling for high-resolution typography, unlike the strict native pixel sprites. At 50% export size, tagline text is approximately 15.5px; render at 52% or larger to meet a 16px text target. Display below that size requires a separately rendered larger tagline. Keep the scene background, title, tagline, and menu choices independently editable.

All exports remain unapproved art samples. The title has not been legally or commercially cleared.
