# Foyer tile atlas

Original native-grid review samples. `foyer_atlas_here.png` and `foyer_atlas_almost.png` share one 256×256 layout with 16×16 cells. `foyer_atlas.json` documents tile indices, individual object rectangles and anchors. Unused atlas cells are transparent. These assets have not been imported into Godot or approved by the user.

Rebuild with `python art_staging/source/draw_environment.py` from the repository root. The helper authors and exports art only.
